Summary:
Handlebar mount for 25.8mm diameter handlebars. Needs four off threaded M3 inserts (ones I used are about 4.5mm OD) and four off M3 bolts.Print with the clamping face of the Barmin flat to the bed with supports enabled. For strength I use 3 walls and 35% gyroid infill in Cura.
