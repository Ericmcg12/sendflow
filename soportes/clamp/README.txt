Handlebar bracket for Modular Mounting System or Moon lights by switch87 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3342843

Summary:
This bracket can be used in combination with my Modular Mounting System conversion to be able to mount all kinds of assessors on your bike with only one clamp on your steering bar. The bracket is based on and partly compatible with the original one delivered with Moon bike lights.The bracket has a separate mounting body (slide-body.stl) to make it possible to horizontally turn the mounted assessor. For Modular Mounting SystemTo use it as a universal modular mounting system, you do not need to print the lockspring and slide-lid, instead you will have to print the conversion found here: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3350991For Moon light ownerssome measurements are not the same as the original bracket, but the slider lid and lock-spring are compatible.there are 2 locksprings, the original that needs a metal spring to work and one with a bending part that has the function of the spring.
